# Short description
Manage trips, drivers, vehicles and live GPS tracking for your truck fleet.

# Full description
CargoTracker helps transport businesses run their truck fleet from one app. Plan and assign trips, track drivers live on a map, keep vehicle papers up to date, and see how your fleet is doing.

Built for three roles: admins, managers and drivers. Each person sees only what they need.

FOR MANAGERS AND ADMINS
• Dashboard: see active, completed and pending trips at a glance, with your most recent trips
• Trip management: create, edit and assign trips with dates, drivers, vehicles and plants. Search and filter trips by status
• Live map: see where active drivers are in real time
• Driver management: add drivers with photos and profile details, and link them to their accounts
• Vehicle management: keep a record of every vehicle, with a service log for each one
• Fuel and maintenance: log fuel fill-ups, services, costs and odometer readings
• Fleet health: get reminders before insurance, permits, PUC, RC, fitness certificates, driver licences or services expire or fall due
• Documents: upload and store driver and vehicle documents in one place
• Plants: manage your loading and unloading locations
• Reports: view trip analytics by date range and by driver
• User roles: choose who is an admin, a manager or a driver

FOR DRIVERS
• See your assigned trips and their details
• Get notified when a new trip is assigned to you
• Share your location while you are on a trip, so your manager knows where the load is

PRIVACY AND SECURITY
• Sign in with email and password. You can reset your password with a one-time code sent to your email
• Sensitive identity documents can only be seen by admins
• Drivers can turn location sharing off at any time
• You can delete your account at any time from Profile in the app

CargoTracker is a good fit for small and mid-sized trucking companies, transporters and logistics teams that want to stop running their fleet on phone calls and spreadsheets.
