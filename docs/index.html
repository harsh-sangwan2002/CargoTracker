CargoTracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

Data we collect Sharing Retention Delete your account

CargoTracker ("the app", "we", "us") is a fleet and cargo management app used by transport companies, their managers and their drivers to record trips, track vehicles and log fuel. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete your account.

The app is intended for use by a company's staff. Your employer (the company that added you to CargoTracker) decides how the trip and vehicle records it keeps in the app are used.

Data we collect

Data Why we collect it
Account details: email address, password (stored hashed), role (driver, manager or admin) Signing in and controlling what each user can see and do.
Profile details: full name, phone number, address, age Identifying staff and drivers to their company.
Driver identity documents: Aadhaar number, PAN number, vehicle owned, profile photo Driver verification, which the company records when it adds a driver.
Precise location (GPS) Showing a driver's live position to their company's managers while trip tracking is turned on. Location is only collected while the app is open and tracking is on.
Trip and fuel records: truck, route, cargo, quantity, times, distance, fuel quantity and cost, odometer readings, fuel receipt photos Operating the fleet and keeping the company's trip and fuel history.
Photos from the camera or gallery Only when you choose to attach a driver photo or receipt.
Device push token Sending trip and status notifications to your device.

We do not collect contacts, messages, call logs, browsing history or advertising IDs, and the app shows no ads.

How we use your data

  • To provide the app's features: sign-in, trips, live tracking, fuel logs and notifications.
  • To send one-time codes by email for password resets and account deletion.
  • To keep the service secure, for example rate-limiting sign-in codes and keeping an audit log of changes to records.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising or profiling.

Who we share data with

Your data is visible to authorised managers and admins of the company you work with. We also use these service providers to run the app, and they process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage and server functions. All app data is stored here.
  • Resend: sends sign-in, password reset and account deletion emails (your email address and the code).
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: deliver push notifications (your device push token and the notification text).
  • Google Maps: displays maps in the app.
  • Sentry: crash and performance reports (error details, device and app version, and your account ID, but not your email address).

We may also disclose data if required by law.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Access to records is restricted on the server, so each user can only access the data their role permits. Passwords and one-time codes are never stored in plain text.

How long we keep data

  • Account, profile and driver identity data are kept until you delete your account or your company removes you.
  • Your last known location is replaced each time a new one is recorded, and is deleted with your account.
  • Password reset codes expire after 10 minutes. Other one-time codes expire shortly after they are sent and can be used only once.
  • Trip, fuel and audit records belong to your company and are kept after your account is deleted, so that the company keeps its operating history. Your link to these records is removed, but a driver name typed into a trip record may remain part of that record.

Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit your profile details in the app on the Profile screen.
  • You can stop location sharing at any time by turning off trip tracking, or by removing the location permission in your device settings.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete Account) or on this page, below.
  • For any other request about your data, contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

Children

CargoTracker is a workplace app and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.

Delete your CargoTracker account

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your sign-in account and profile (name, phone, address)
  • your driver record (Aadhaar, PAN, photo, vehicle)
  • your location data, notifications and device push tokens

Your company's trip and fuel records are kept, with your account removed from them (see How long we keep data). Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.
